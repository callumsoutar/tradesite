A website for a New Zealand trade business is usually priced as a one-off build, sometimes with a separate hosting or maintenance fee afterwards. The number on its own is not very useful until you know how many pages you get, who writes the words, and whether the domain and hosting are included.

## What the price is usually buying

A small trade site needs a home page, a way to explain the services, a way to show work, and a way to call or send a message. Mobile layout matters, because a lot of these visits happen on a phone. Basic on-page SEO means each page has its own title, a clear heading, and copy about the actual trade and location.

That is a different job from a large custom web application, and it should not be priced like one. It is also a different job from a template you finish yourself.

## A practical way to compare quotes

Ask each quote these questions:

- How many pages are included, and what happens if you need another one?
- Is the wording included, or are you expected to write it?
- Do you own the finished site and the domain?
- Is hosting included, or is it a monthly extra?
- Are the prices inclusive of GST?
- Can you see a draft before you pay?
